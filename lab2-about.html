About Lab 2

In this lab, I learned how to effectively use links and tables in HTML to build a more structured and navigable website. I practiced linking pages within my site using relative paths, as well as linking to external websites using absolute paths. I also created HTML tables, ensuring they were properly structured and included accessibility attributes like scope and aria-label to improve their usability for individuals with disabilities. By focusing on accessibility, I gained a better understanding of how to make my web content more inclusive, ensuring it can be accessed and navigated easily by everyone.
