About Me

Hey! I'm Adrian Peterson, a passionate game designer and Roblox developer with 3+ years of experience creating immersive worlds and interactive gameplay experiences. I specialize in building engaging multiplayer games using Roblox Studio and Lua scripting.

From castle siege battles to galactic space shooters, I love bringing creative ideas to life. I'm currently pursuing my degree in IT while continuing to develop games that players love. My goal is to join a professional game studio and contribute to large-scale projects.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me studying game mechanics, exploring new Roblox experiences, or sketching out concepts for my next big project.
